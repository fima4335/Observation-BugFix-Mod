# Known Issues in Observation

## Resolution & Display

| Issue | Severity | Status | Workaround |
|-------|----------|--------|------------|
| Black bars (letterboxing) on all aspect ratios | High | Community fix available | Rose black bars removal mod |
| Resolution list limited after v1.17 | High | Community fix available | Ultrawide patcher by Rose |
| Resolution resets on launch | Medium | Registry workaround | Set Screenmanager values in registry |
| Controls hints cut off on ultrawide | Low | Unresolved | Temporarily use narrower resolution |
| 16:10 support incomplete | Medium | Partial | Registry + black bars mod |

## Stability

| Issue | Severity | Status | Workaround |
|-------|----------|--------|------------|
| Crash on startup | High | Partial | Compatibility mode Windows 8, disable overlays, Steam Input for controllers |
| Crash with Xbox controller | Medium | Partial | Configure controller via Steam |
| NVIDIA Overlay interference | Medium | Workaround | Disable GeForce Experience overlay |

## Settings

| Issue | Severity | Status | Workaround |
|-------|----------|--------|------------|
| VSync inverted | Low | Known | Toggle opposite of desired state |
| Settings not always persisting | Medium | Partial | Registry method |

## Engine Notes

- Engine: **Unity 2017** (Mono)
- Latest public patch: **v1.17** (August 2020)
- Developers (No Code) acknowledged some resolution issues but no further public patches after 1.17.
